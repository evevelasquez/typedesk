Open Brain privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Open Brain is a personal tool with one user, Eve Velasquez. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with that information.

What Open Brain accesses

With Eve's permission through Google sign-in, Open Brain reads and drafts messages in her Gmail account and reads and updates her Google Calendar. It accesses no other person's account.

How it uses that information

Open Brain uses Gmail and Calendar data only to help Eve manage her own email, schedule, and tasks. It stores the notes and tasks it creates in a private database that only Eve controls.

Sharing

Open Brain does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Eve can revoke Open Brain's access at any time from her Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.
